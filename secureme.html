Protect Your findMe Account

SecureMe helps buyers and sellers recognise scams, report suspicious activity and protect their personal information.

Security advice and reporting tools are provided here. This page does not certify that a seller, product or payment is safe.

🚨 Never share these details

  • Banking passwords or account login credentials.
  • Card PINs, CVVs or complete payment-card numbers.
  • One-time passwords (OTPs) or banking approval codes.
  • Your findMe password with a buyer, seller or support agent.
  • Recovery codes or private authentication tokens.

A legitimate findMe support process should never ask you to disclose your banking password, PIN or OTP.

🔎 Scam Warning Signs

  • A seller demands payment urgently or pressures you to act immediately.
  • Someone sends a suspicious payment-confirmation screenshot.
  • A buyer or seller asks you to open an unexpected payment link.
  • A deal seems unusually cheap and the seller refuses reasonable checks.
  • Someone requests an OTP, banking password or remote access to your device.
  • A seller asks you to pay outside the agreed, trusted payment process.

These signs warrant caution, but their absence does not guarantee that a transaction is legitimate.

📝 Report a Suspicious Listing or Scam

Describe what happened without including passwords, banking information, identity-document numbers or other unnecessary personal information.

Privacy notice: this demonstration prepares a report locally in your browser. It does not send the report to findMe or save it in a server database. You must submit it through an appropriate reporting channel.

💳 Payment Safety

  • Verify that a payment has actually arrived in your account.
  • Do not rely on screenshots, emails or SMS messages as proof of payment.
  • Use a reputable payment provider for online transactions.
  • Check the website address before entering payment information.
  • Never ask customers to send banking passwords or OTPs to your store.

findMe does not process payments through this page.

🔐 If You Think Your Account Has Been Compromised

  1. Use a trusted device to change the affected account password.
  2. Contact your bank immediately if you suspect payment fraud.
  3. Contact your email provider if your email account may be compromised.
  4. Preserve relevant messages and transaction references.
  5. Report the incident to the appropriate service or authority.
